This entry pitches **FridgeCourt**: an AI app that subpoenas your own fridge, puts your midnight snacks on trial before a jury of twelve neighbours, and fines you in crypto for eating leftovers after 11pm. It lands because it sounds exactly like a real founder pitch — AI, crypto, a jury mechanic, a confident product name — while solving a problem nobody has ever had: nobody on earth has wished their leftovers were legally actionable, and nobody needs twelve neighbours voting on a sandwich at midnight. The more specific the legal machinery gets, the more perfectly pointless the whole thing becomes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the pitch sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
